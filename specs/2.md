NOT IN THIS PHASE: Healer heal/poison, Poison, Crown Summon, deployment. Healers exist
on the board but cannot act except to move, and they never count as attackers.

PIECE STATE
- Each piece on the board tracks current hp and current armor (start from its definition).
- A piece is removed the moment its hp <= 0 (even if armor is left).

TURN MODEL (attacking is a turn action)
- Moving NEVER attacks and never triggers attacks.
- On your turn you do exactly ONE action: move a piece OR attack with one piece.
- A piece "has a target" if an enemy is attackable by it (see Targeting below).
  Healers are excluded: they never count as having a target.
- A piece is "threatened" if any enemy non-Healer piece could currently attack it.
- FORCED ENGAGEMENT: if any of your pieces has a target, you may only (a) attack with a
  piece that has a target, or (b) move one of your threatened pieces (e.g. to retreat).
  Any other move is not allowed. If none of your pieces has a target, move freely.
- After an attack or move, the turn passes to the other side.

TARGETING (pure function, unit-testable)
- Attack directions: "Front" = only the single straight line forward (Blue forward = row
  increasing, Red forward = row decreasing). "Front/Diag" = forward line plus all four
  diagonals (forward and backward). "All" = all 8 directions.
- Walk outward from the attacker in each allowed direction up to its range. The FIRST
  piece found is the only candidate: if it is an enemy it is a target; if it is a friend
  or the line is empty, nothing in that direction. River and bridge squares do NOT block
  attacks.

DAMAGE
- Normal attack: damage = attacker's atk. Armor absorbs first; remaining damage hits hp.
- Mage attack is Burn: damage = atk (2) goes straight to hp and ignores armor.
- Armor never regenerates. Remove the piece when hp <= 0.
- If a Crown is removed, the game ends: show the winner and a "New game" button that
  resets the board. Disable all board input while the game is over.
- Sanity check: a fresh Phantom (atk 3) hitting a fresh Crown (hp1, armor1) kills it.
  A fresh Scepter (atk 2) also kills a fresh Crown.

- When it is your turn and you select your own piece:
