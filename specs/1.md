TASK: Phase 1 of "The Bored Game" in Flutter: a playable board with movable pieces only.
Reference: the_bored_game_spec.md (read only Sections 2, 3 and 4). Do not ask me
questions; use the assumptions below. Keep changes minimal: no new packages, no state
management library, no refactoring of existing files, no extra docs.

SCOPE (phase 1 only)
- Single screen with a 13-row x 11-column board (columns a-k, rows 1-13).
- Row 1 is at the bottom (Blue), row 13 at the top (Red). Show coordinate labels.
- Hot-seat play: Blue moves first, then turns alternate. One move per turn.
- NO attacks, HP/Armor, poison, healing or summon yet.

THEME (dark, flat colors, no gradients, no images, easy on the eyes)
- App background: #121212. Normal cells: #1E2126 (one flat color is fine).
- River cells (a7, d7, h7, k7): ocean blue #1F4E79.
- Bridge cells (b7, c7, e7, f7, g7, i7, j7): wooden brown/orange #8B5E34.
- Blue pieces: #6EA8FF text. Red pieces: #FF7B7B text.
- Selected piece: bright outline. Legal destinations: small dot or soft highlight.

PIECES
- Show each piece as a text abbreviation: Tnk, Hlr, Arc, Mge, Kn, Phn, Crn, Scp.
- Define pieces in ONE data structure (type, name, abbreviation, move distance).
  Also store atk, range, direction, hp, armor now so later phases need no rewrite,
  but do not use them yet. Values come from the spec's piece table.
- Starting positions: use the sample layout from the spec for Blue (rows 1-2), but with
  the Scepter at k1 and the Phantom at a1. Red is Blue rotated 180 degrees (row r becomes
  14-r, column c becomes mirrored), so Red Phantom is k13 and Red Scepter is a13.
  Fixed squares: Phantom a1/k13, Scepter k1/a13.

MOVEMENT RULES (implement exactly)
- Tap a piece of the player whose turn it is, then tap a highlighted square to move.
- Move distance: 1x1 = 1 square, 2x2 = up to 2, 3x3 = up to 3, 4x4 = up to 4,
  in any of the 8 directions (straight or diagonal).
- No jumping over any piece (friend or enemy). Cannot land on an occupied square.
  No capture by moving.
- No piece may ever stand on a river square, not even the Phantom.
- Only the Phantom can cross row 7 without a bridge: it may jump over a river square
  as part of its move, but never land on it. It may also walk onto a bridge instead.
- All other pieces can only cross row 7 by walking through a bridge square. A piece
  standing on a bridge blocks that bridge for everyone.
- Moving a piece ends the turn.

ARCHITECTURE (keep it small)
- 3 files at most: piece definitions + board constants, game state with move
  generation (a plain ChangeNotifier or setState is fine), and the board UI.
- Put move generation in a pure function so it can be unit tested.
